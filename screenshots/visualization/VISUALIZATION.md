## Lap Times Table

Table representing useful data regarding the lap time and time taken to reach the next checkpoint.

- **Total race time** total time taken in the entire race.
- **Average lap time** average time taken for each lap.
- **Average checkpoint split** average time taken to reach the next checkpoint.
- **Fastest lap** quickest time a lap has been completed in the entire race.
- **Slowest lap** slowest time a lap has been completed in the entire race.

![Table2](screenshots/visualization/stats_table2.png)

## Off-Road Table

Table giving informations about how often player goes off-road and how long does player goes off-road each time. Time is calculated by the delta of the time at which they went off road and time at which they enter back into the track.

- **Times went off road** total number of times player have went off-road.
- **Average duration** average time of how long the player goes off-road each time.
- **Shortest excursion** shortest instance of player going off-road.
- **Longest excursion** longest instance of player going off-road.

![Table3](screenshots/visualization/stats_table3.png)
